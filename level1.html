Swayam Parichayaḥ

Master the art of introducing yourself and asking questions.

✨

1. Observe & Absorb

Listen to the dialogues. Pay close attention to how the question word changes based on who is being asked.

💡 Grammar Note: The pronoun 'I' (अहम्) and 'You' (त्वम्) remain the same for everyone. However, to ask "Who?", use कः for a male and का for a female.
👦 ➡️ 👦 Boy asking a Boy
अहं रामः। त्वं कः?
I am Rama. Who are you?
👦 ➡️ 👧 Boy asking a Girl
अहं रामः। त्वं का?
I am Rama. Who are you?
👧 ➡️ 👧 Girl asking a Girl
अहं सीता। त्वं का?
I am Sita. Who are you?
